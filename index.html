Welcome to our community!

We proudly serve homemade syrups, locally roasted coffee and baked goods, and provide a cozy space to gather and find community.

Made with Care

Great coffee brings people in, but it's the care behind every cup that keeps them coming back. We believe the best things are made close to home, which is why we proudly serve locally roasted coffee, house-made syrups (plus Rhode Island's favorite, Autocrat), and a thoughtfully curated selection of loose-leaf teas. Our pastries are baked from scratch by a talented local baker. As we grow, we're excited to expand our shelves with a curated collection of goods from other local makers and small businesses. Every visit is a chance to support your community. Take a look at our menu to see what's brewing and find your next favorite drink or treat.

Stay Awhile

Whether you're catching up with friends, meeting a study group, settling in to work, or enjoying a quiet morning coffee, we designed our space to feel comfortable, welcoming, and easy to linger in. You'll find cozy couches for long conversations, larger tables for gathering, and intimate spots for a coffee date or solo afternoon. As you look around, don't miss the artwork on our walls. Each month we feature a different local artist, giving them a space to share their work.

Community First

We believe a great cafe is more than a place to grab coffee--it's a place where people feel like they belong. Our goal is to create a welcoming third space where neighbors become friends, conversations happen naturally, and everyone has a seat at the table. From weekly game nights, crafting meetups, and mahjong groups to special one-time events, there's always a reason to gather, connect, and be part of the community. Whether you're joining us for an event, meeting a friend, or simply looking for a comfortable place to spend some time, we're glad you're here. Be sure to check out our event calendar to see what's happening next.
