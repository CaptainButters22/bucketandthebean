Welcome to our community!

We proudly serve homemade syrups, locally roasted coffee and baked goods, and provide a cozy space to gather and find community.

Taste

We pride ourselves on keeping things local as much as possible. We serve all house-made syrups (and Autocrat), locally roasted beans, and a variety of looseleaf teas. All our pastries are baked from scratch by a local baker. As our menu expands, we plan to expand our retail to curate a small collection of other local businesses.

Space

Our space was designed with you in mind; we have space for larger group meet-ups, couch seating for gossip sessions, and 2-person tables for getting some work done or a morning coffee date. When you visit, make sure to check out the art on the walls. Every month, we highlight a new local artist, and the pieces are often for sale.

Gather

We are more than a cafe, but a community third-space. Everyone who walks in should feel welcomed. Toward this end, we host a variety of weekly community events, as well as fun one-off events, so don't forget to check out our calendar of events!
